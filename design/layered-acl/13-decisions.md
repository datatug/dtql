## Decisions that must not be left to divergent implementation agents

Approve C1–C6 together after the independent reviews. In particular: exact new version namespace; stricter public-profile query restrictions vs legacy DALgo; field deny intent as allow-list; public visibility meaning and private admin grant; remote evidence/CAS limitations; policy admission/revocation semantics; and sample result/limits. Reconciler should distinguish concrete contradictions requiring edits from these intentional trade-offs. Accepting a recommendation is not permission to implement the entire future scope.
